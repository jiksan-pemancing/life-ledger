# Life Ledger

A life and money simulator you play in the browser. You start at 18 with $3,000 and live one month at a time until a random age between 60 and 120. Jobs, bills, markets, housing, family and health all compound.

## Play

Open the game in any browser. It works on phones too: use **Add to Home Screen** to install it like an app. Progress saves automatically on your device.

Or run it locally by opening `index.html`. There is no build step and no dependencies.

## How it works

- **Town:** click a building to manage that part of your life.
  - School: education
  - Job Centre or workplace: career and side gigs
  - Bank: debt, mortgage and taxes
  - Home: housing and monthly budget
  - Stock Exchange: investing
  - Clinic: health
  - Park: family
- **Time:** advance 1 month, 3 months, 1 year, or until something happens. Long skips pause on big events and decisions.
- **Economy:** it cycles through expansion, slowdown, recession and recovery. Stock returns have fat tails and volatility clusters. Interest rates and inflation move with the cycle.
- **Money:** income streams include salary, bonus, side gigs, room rent, dividends, pension and your partner's pay. There is an itemized budget, credit card and student debt, monthly tax withholding with an April settlement, and fixed, 5-year fixed and variable mortgages.
- **Life:** partner, children, divorce, parent care, medical emergencies, inheritances, scams and temptations.
- **Ending:** when you die, you get a grade based on happiness, financial security, legacy and health.

The economics are generic and simplified. They are not financial advice.
